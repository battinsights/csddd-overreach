Overreach? Companies in Scope of the EU Corporate Sustainability Due Diligence Directive (CSDDD)

A selection of large companies that meet or approach the directive's thresholds: €1.5 billion of EU turnover for non-EU firms, or 5,000 employees and €1.5 billion worldwide turnover for EU firms. Bubble size reflects approximate annual revenue. Roughly a third of in-scope corporate groups are headquartered outside the European Union.

Potential impacts
01
Fines and scope
Fines reach 3% of worldwide turnover. Non-EU companies are covered on €1.5 billion of EU sales alone, wherever they are headquartered.
02
Small suppliers
Due diligence demands cascade to SMEs through contracts, questionnaires, and audits that many lack the resources to meet.
03
Costs and prices
Compliance and supply chain restructuring costs can pass through to prices for energy, fuel, and consumer goods.
04
Unintended effects
Firms may exit hard-to-document suppliers rather than improve them, shifting trade without improving conditions on the ground.
